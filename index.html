Field notes

I'm a second-year Engineering Physics student at UBC with three-plus years of professional work behind me already — bartending, pharmacy, and now electrical systems for a Formula SAE race car. I like environments where precision and pace both matter: a full bar on a Friday night isn't so different from a wiring harness with a deadline. Outside of coursework, I design and build hardware with SolidWorks and Altair, and I gravitate toward roles where I end up teaching or leading — organizing exam prep for 120 classmates, captaining a varsity team, presenting technical work to teammates who don't share my background.
