Formula UBC Racing — Electrical Subteam

Dashboard Enclosure

A 3D-printed housing for the driver dashboard electronics on UBC's 2025/26 Formula SAE car.

The dashboard sits at the intersection of almost every subsystem on the car: it has to house the driver display and switches, route wiring from multiple harnesses, and bolt cleanly into the chassis without interfering with the steering column or bodywork. I designed the enclosure that holds all of it together.

Packaging was the hard constraint. The enclosure needed clearance for connectors on every side, mounting points shared with neighbouring subsystems, and enough structural rigidity to survive vibration on track — all inside a footprint dictated by the cockpit, not the electronics. I iterated through several SolidWorks revisions, checking fit against the wiring harness and the surrounding bodywork at each pass before handing the part off for 3D printing and in-car fit testing.

It's a small part with an outsized number of constraints touching it — a good summary of what electrical subteam work looks like on a Formula SAE team.
