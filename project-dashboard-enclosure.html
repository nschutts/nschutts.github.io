Dashboard Enclosure

A 3D-printed housing for dashboard electronics on UBC's 2025/26 FSAE car.

In my first year on Formula UBC, I designed the dashboard enclosure for our 2025/26 Formula SAE car. The enclosure houses the driver displays, display selector switch, LED RPM indicator strip, and the car's start button, and is bolted directly onto the steering column.

The main challenge was packaging. Space was limited by the chassis and steering geometry, so every connector and mounting point inside the unit had to fit around constraints set by other subsystems. Those constraints also changed more than once during the design and manufacturing process. As neighboring parts were redesigned, I had to change mounting geometry, update connector clearances, and remanufacture the enclosure.

Working on this project taught me how to design for 3D printing at a specific infill and wall thickness, laser cut custom gaskets, prepare professional engineering drawings, and adapt to rapidly changing requirements on tight timelines. I experienced firsthand the importance of consultation with other subsystems to limit the amount of work that needs to be redone, and how to convey my ideas effectively to my peers and coworkers. The final project was a success, and FSAE judges were impressed by our dashboard during the design review event at FSAE Michigan
