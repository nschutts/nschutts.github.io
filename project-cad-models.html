Battery & Connector CAD Models

SolidWorks models of the car's battery and connector, built from hand measurements and used for packaging in the top-level assembly.

The challenge:
The car's top-level assembly needed accurate models of the battery and its connector so other subsystems could be packaged around them.

The approach. I measured the physical battery and connector with calipers and recorded their dimensions. I then built both models in SolidWorks from those measurements, with a 1mm tolerance.

The result. The completed models were added to the car's top-level assembly, where the team used them to check clearances and place neighbouring components.
